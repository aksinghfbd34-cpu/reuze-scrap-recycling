Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about scrap rates, doorstep weighing, and payouts.

How does the free doorstep scrap pickup work?

Booking takes less than 60 seconds on our website or app. Select the items you want to sell, choose your preferred date and time slot, and provide your address. Our verified Scrap Hero arrives with an ISO-certified digital weighing scale, weighs your scrap in front of you, and makes an instant cash or UPI payment immediately before leaving.

Are your digital weighing scales accurate and certified?

Yes, 100%! All Reuze Scrap Heroes carry Department of Legal Metrology (Govt of India) calibrated electronic digital weighing scales. You can verify the tare weight and inspect the scale on the spot before weighing.

Is there any minimum quantity or pickup charge?

Doorstep pickup is 100% FREE! There are zero hidden charges or conveyance fees. For residential pickups, we recommend a minimum scrap value of ₹200 (approx. 10 kg of mixed scrap or 1 small electronic appliance) for hassle-free slot assignment.

How does vehicle scrapping work under the Govt RVSF policy?

Under the Motor Vehicles Rules, we handle end-to-end scrapping at Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facilities (RVSF). We pick up your end-of-life vehicle with free towing, issue a valid Certificate of Deposit (CD) for road tax rebates on new car purchases, and handle complete RTO de-registration.

What payment modes are supported upon pickup?

We support instant UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), IMPS Bank Transfer, and on-the-spot Cash. You can also choose to donate your scrap earnings directly to our partnered Green Forestation NGOs.
